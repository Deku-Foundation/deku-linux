# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Commitment

Deku Linux aims to be a respectful, constructive, and evidence-driven open-source project. Participation should be free from harassment and discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Explain technical decisions with evidence and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep discussions focused on project goals, user safety, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to maintainers through a verified contact channel on the organization profile. Include relevant context and links. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially where practical and may remove content or restrict participation proportionately.

This policy applies to project spaces and public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
