01 / Principles

Less noise.
More intention.

Good systems come from disciplined choices. Deku aims to reuse mature technology where it helps, and innovate only where it matters.

01
↗

Built on proven work

Learn from established Linux projects, preserve their licenses, and avoid reinventing components without a measurable reason.

02
⌁

Security by process

Reviewable changes, isolated builds, traceable sources, and release controls are foundations — not marketing claims.

03
◫

Reliability you can test

Measure upgrades, recovery, and reproducibility before promising them. Keep the system understandable and maintainable.

02 / Technical direction

Borrow wisely.
Build deliberately.

Deku is evaluating mature packaging and system technologies alongside ideas from declarative and reproducible build systems. These are research directions, not final architecture decisions.
